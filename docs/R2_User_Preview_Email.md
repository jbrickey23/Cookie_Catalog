Subject: Cookie Catalog: what we’re working on next

Hi everyone,

Cookie Catalog’s first release is live, and we’re working on a fast-follow update based on how you use it.

Here’s what’s planned for R2:

- Small usability improvements, including clearer navigation, sign-out and easier access to Undo.
- A Request a change option that captures what you were viewing and lets you describe a bug, improvement or idea. Requests will be saved privately and reviewed for upcoming releases.
- A photo inbox: upload several photos, let background processing prepare suggestions, then review and approve a new cutter or updates to an existing one. You stay in control of what gets saved.
- More dependable releases and recovery, with automated checks and a brief editing pause during updates.

These features are in development; there’s no release date yet. We’ll share what’s ready as it passes testing. Until then, please keep using the production catalog and send us anything that feels confusing or could work better.

Open the catalog: https://cookie-catalog-prod.j-brickey.workers.dev/

Thanks for helping shape the next release!
